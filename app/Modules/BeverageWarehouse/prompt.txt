6. Depot Pricing
Depot/warehouse customers may buy smaller quantities such as 5 or 10 packs. Depot price differs by SKU and can also differ by quantity. Extend the current pricing mechanism to support quantity tiers: SKU, sales channel, minimum quantity, maximum quantity, price, customer class, depot/location, effective date, expiry date and status.
7. Diversion Pricing
Diversion means selling/diverting stock to another distributor. The receiving distributor must be captured on the existing customer/distributor record. Use the active diversion price for the SKU and date. Allow management to configure a minimum diversion threshold. Any transaction below it requires permission, approval/reason and audit history.
8. Rebate / Slab Engine
Extend cost calculation so Invoice Price is not treated as the only cost. The system must calculate an Effective Landing Cost using the supplier rebate/slab rules.
PET 60CL working example:
•	Invoice Price: ₦4,150.00
•	Rebate Base Price: ₦4,207.07
•	Example slabs: 3%, 2.5%, 2%
•	Adjustment factor used in supplied example: 92.5%
Rebate Value = Rebate Base Price × Slab % × Adjustment Factor
Effective Landing Cost = Invoice Price − Rebate Value
Slab	Approx. Rebate	Effective Landing	Depot @ ₦4,100	Diversion @ ₦4,050
3%	₦116.75	₦4,033.25	+₦66.75	+₦16.75
2.5%	₦97.29	₦4,052.71	+₦47.29	−₦2.71
2%	₦77.83	₦4,072.17	+₦27.83	−₦22.17
IMPORTANT: Make the rebate percentage, rebate base, adjustment factor, formula type and effective dates configurable. Do not permanently call the 92.5% factor 'VAT' until management/accounting confirms its exact treatment.

13. Profitability
•	Invoice Margin = Selling Price − Invoice Price
•	Expected Rebate Benefit = rebate/slab allocation
•	Promotion Benefit = qualifying promotion allocation
•	Effective Product Margin = Selling Price − Effective Landing Cost
•	Operational/Net Margin = Effective Product Margin − configured direct costs, if the current finance model supports this
Management must be able to distinguish 'below invoice but profitable after rebate' from a genuine sale below effective cost.
